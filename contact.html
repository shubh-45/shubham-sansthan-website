Ways to help

Four things that genuinely make a difference

We have never run a fundraising campaign. But there are useful things people ask about, so here they are.

Volunteer with a programme

Awareness camps, street theatre, enrolment drives and health information all run on people who show up. If you are in or near Barmer and have time, tell us what you are good at.

Partner with us

We are empanelled with the Government of India's Song & Drama Division and registered with NACO. Departments, agencies and other organisations looking for a delivery partner in Barmer district can write to us directly.

Send us the record

Photographs, newspaper cuttings, certificates and programme reports from twenty-five years of work are scattered across many people's houses. If you have any of it, send it — it belongs on this website.

Support the work

If you would like to contribute to a specific programme, please call or email us first so we can tell you exactly what it will be used for and give you proper receipts.
