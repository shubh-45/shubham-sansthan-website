## 3. Written new for this website

These were **not** on the old site. They are ordinary connective and framing copy, written
to hold the facts together. Nothing here invents an achievement, a number, or a partner —
but you should still read it and make it sound like you.

- All headlines and section introductions.
- The "Who we are" and "Why this website exists" passages.
- The "Shiksha · Sanskar · Seva" explanations (`about.html`) — an interpretation of your
  own motto and emblem. **Please check this reads correctly to you.**
- The nine programme descriptions on `our-work.html`. The *facts* are from the original
  site or from your mission statement; the surrounding explanation is new.
- The "Track record" timeline on `our-work.html` — real events, in a plausible order.
  **It has no dates on it deliberately**, because the original site gave none. Add them if
  you know them.
- The "Ways to help" section on `contact.html`.
- The "Why we set this down" note on `barmer.html`.

## 4. Things you should add

- [ ] **Real photographs.** This is the biggest gap. Twenty-five years of camps,
      performances, drives and relief work with almost no pictures on the site.
      Photographs of actual work will do more than any amount of design.
- [ ] **Names of office bearers** — president, secretary, treasurer.
- [ ] **Real numbers** — villages covered, camps held, people reached. Even rough,
      honest figures are far stronger than adjectives.
- [ ] **Scanned certificates** of the district-level honours.
- [ ] **PAN, 12A/80G registration numbers** if the Sansthan has them — donors will ask,
      and 80G makes donations tax-deductible.
- [ ] **A Hindi version of the site.** This site is in English by request. Most of the
      people the Sansthan serves read Hindi. A `/hi/` folder with the same pages in Hindi
      would be worth doing.
